# DPDPFlow Platform Facts & Architecture

## Company Overview
- **Company**: RegulationFlow Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
- **Product**: DPDPFlow (https://dpdpflow.com)
- **Tagline**: Purpose-Built Indian DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Automation.
- **Target Audience**: DPOs, CISOs, Heads of Legal, CTOs, and Founders at Indian enterprises and high-growth startups (50–5,000 employees) across Fintech, Healthtech, Edtech, E-commerce, and SaaS.
- **Demo Link**: https://dpdpflow.com/demo

## Core Modules & Value Proposition

### 1. Consent Architecture & Section 5 Notice Management
- Dynamic multi-lingual itemised consent notice builder supporting all 22 Eighth Schedule Indian languages.
- Direct API connector for registered Consent Managers (operational by November 2026).
- Real-time consent revocation synchronization across databases, CRM, and marketing pipelines.

### 2. Breach Notification & Section 8 Safeguards
- Automated 72-hour incident response workflow for intimating the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and impacted Data Principals under Section 8(6).
- Automated risk scoring and root-cause evidence bundle generator to mitigate potential statutory penalties (penalties can reach up to ₹250 crore depending on the breach).
- Continuous posture scanning of databases and cloud data stores to enforce reasonable security safeguards under Section 8(5).

### 3. Data Minimisation & Automated Retention (Section 8(7))
- Out-of-the-box 90-day retention policies, automated purpose expiry detection, and cryptographic deletion verification.
- Enforces strict right-to-erasure workflows under Section 11(2) without requiring manual SQL scripts.

### 4. DPO Governance & Tamper-Evident Audit Trails (Section 10)
- Single-pane DPO command centre for Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs).
- DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment) templates pre-calibrated to MeitY guidelines.
- Immutable hash-chained audit logs ready for regulatory submission in inquiries or board audits.

### 5. Sovereign Deployment & Zero Data Exfiltration (Section 16)
- Runs completely within Indian cloud regions (AWS Mumbai / Hyderabad, GCP Delhi / Mumbai, Azure Pune / Chennai) or on-premise.
- Eliminates non-compliant cross-border transfer exposure under Section 16.

### 6. Fast Time-to-Value
- Deploys in under 14 days with zero disruptions to existing production infrastructure.
- Fraction of the cost of building custom privacy engineering tools or hiring dedicated privacy consulting teams.
